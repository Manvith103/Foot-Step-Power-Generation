
# ⚡ Foot Step Power Generation System Using Piezoelectric Sensors

## 📌 Project Overview

The Foot Step Power Generation System is a project that demonstrates the conversion of mechanical energy generated by human footsteps into electrical energy using piezoelectric sensors.

When pressure is applied to the piezoelectric sensors through footsteps, they generate an electrical output. This project explores the use of human mechanical energy as a potential source of electrical energy.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Generate electrical energy from human footsteps.
- Understand the working principle of piezoelectric sensors.
- Demonstrate mechanical-to-electrical energy conversion.
- Explore alternative and renewable energy harvesting methods.
- Study potential applications of footstep-based energy generation.

## ⚙️ Working Principle

The system operates based on the piezoelectric effect.

1. A person applies mechanical pressure by stepping on the platform.
2. The pressure acts on the piezoelectric sensors.
3. The sensors generate an electrical output.
4. The generated output is processed through the implemented electrical circuit.
5. The electrical energy can be utilized by a suitable load or stored, depending on the system configuration.

## 🔧 Components Used

## 🏗️ Hardware Implementation

The prototype demonstrates the generation of electrical energy through mechanical pressure applied to piezoelectric sensors.

The sensors are arranged within the footstep platform to capture the mechanical energy produced during stepping.
